# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Patients, including people who register another family member, use the system on mobile or desktop to complete and follow an MCU visit.
- Hospital staff operate examination stations, check patients in, manage queues, and record results.
- Healthcare professionals review consolidated MCU results and verify completeness before release.
- Management monitors service time, bottlenecks, capacity, satisfaction, and complaints.

## Product Purpose

ISLAH digitizes the Medical Check Up process at RSUD Haji Provinsi Jawa Timur from registration through same-day result delivery. It is intended to reduce repeated identity checks, fragmented station workflows, manual queues, long waits, and lost paper results.

## Positioning

One registration and one queue identity connect the full MCU journey across examination stations, operational tracking, clinical verification, and digital results.

## Operating Context

The product is used during hospital MCU registration, payment, check-in, waiting, examination, result verification, and management review. Patients may be unfamiliar with technology and may still need assistance from hospital staff.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Patient registration, package selection, digital payment, QR check-in, queue estimates, hospital directions, MCU history, digital results, reviews, and complaints.
- Staff worklists, QR check-in, station queues, service timing, examination result entry, and long-duration service capacity.
- Healthcare professional result review and final completeness verification.
- Management reports for response time, turnaround time, queues, bottlenecks, satisfaction, complaints, and service capacity.
- Queue status depends on staff confirmation.
- One patient can hold one MCU package at a time.
- The web product must work on mobile and desktop.
- Patient information must be protected.
- This implementation is a UI prototype with static illustrative data and no backend, authentication, payment, scanning, or data persistence.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: ISLAH.
- Organization: RSUD Haji Provinsi Jawa Timur.
- Tagline: "One Service, Selesai Hasil Hari Ini."
- The interface should be simple, fast, minimalist, and suitable for a healthcare setting.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product requirements: `../design/Digitalisasi MCU RSUD Haji.pdf`.
- Development workflow: `../design/workflow.md`.
- No approved logo, photography, testimonials, or production patient data are provided. The prototype must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the next required action before secondary information.
2. Keep one patient identity and journey visible across every station.
3. Make delays, missing results, and capacity constraints easy to identify.
4. Use familiar language and clear hierarchy for patients with varying digital literacy.
5. Label illustrative prototype data honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface should use clear Indonesian labels, readable contrast, large touch targets, keyboard-visible focus, and responsive layouts. Patient views should remain understandable for users with limited technical familiarity.
